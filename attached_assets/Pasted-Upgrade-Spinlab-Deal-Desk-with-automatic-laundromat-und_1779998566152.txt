Important:
- Do not rebuild the app from scratch.
- Keep the current design, pages, database, CRUD, reminders, brokers, notes, delete/archive/restore, and email features.

--------------------------------
5. SBA / DEBT FORMULAS
--------------------------------

Calculate:

Down Payment =
asking_price * down_payment_percent / 100

Seller Financing Amount =
seller_financing_amount

Loan Amount =
asking_price - down_payment - seller_financing_amount

Closing Costs =
asking_price * closing_cost_percent / 100

Total Cash Needed =
down_payment
+ closing_costs
+ sba_fees
+ working_capital_reserve
+ capex_budget

Bank Monthly Debt Service =
standard amortizing payment using:
loan_amount
interest_rate
amortization_years

Seller Financing Monthly Payment =
standard amortizing payment using:
seller_financing_amount
seller_financing_interest_rate
seller_financing_amortization_years

Total Monthly Debt Service =
bank_monthly_debt_service + seller_financing_monthly_payment

Annual Debt Service =
total_monthly_debt_service * 12

Cash Flow After Debt =
adjusted_sde - annual_debt_service

Monthly Cash Flow After Debt =
cash_flow_after_debt / 12

DSCR =
adjusted_sde / annual_debt_service

Cash-on-Cash Return =
cash_flow_after_debt / total_cash_needed

Break-even Revenue =
total_operating_expenses + annual_debt_service

Break-even Revenue % =
break_even_revenue / gross_revenue

Monthly payment formula:

Monthly Payment =
P * [r(1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n - 1]

Where:
P = principal
r = monthly interest rate
n = total monthly payments

Handle 0% interest safely:
If interest rate is 0, monthly payment = principal / n
