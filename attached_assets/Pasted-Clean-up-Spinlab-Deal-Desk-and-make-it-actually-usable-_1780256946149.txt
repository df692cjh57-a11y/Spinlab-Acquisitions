10. FINANCIAL MATH SAFETY
--------------------------------

Make sure underwriting math is consistent.

Use one shared helper for calculations.

Fix:
- NaN
- Infinity
- undefined
- null
- divide-by-zero
- inconsistent math between table/detail/dashboard

If a metric cannot be calculated, show:
Not enough data

Required calculations:
- Annual rent = monthly rent x 12
- Rent % of gross = annual rent / gross revenue
- Rent per square foot = annual rent / square feet
- Asking multiple = asking price / adjusted SDE
- Max offer = adjusted SDE x target multiple
- Price gap = asking price - max offer
- Down payment = asking price x down payment percent
- Loan amount = asking price - down payment - seller financing amount
- Monthly debt service using amortization formula
- Annual debt service = monthly debt service x 12
- DSCR = adjusted SDE / annual debt service
- Cash-on-cash return = cash flow after debt / total cash needed
- Deal score
- Red flag score
- Suggested offer

Format numbers:
- Currency: $950,000
- Percent: 18.5%
- Multiple: 4.7x
- DSCR: 1.25x
