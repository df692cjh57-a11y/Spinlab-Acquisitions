4. Add automatic calculations

For every deal calculate:

- Annual rent = monthly rent x 12
- Asking multiple = asking price / adjusted net income
- Rent as % of gross = annual rent / gross revenue
- Rent per square foot = annual rent / square footage

Format these properly:
- Currency with dollar signs
- Percentages with %
- Multiples with “x”

Example:
- $950,000
- 18.4%
- 4.7x

5. Add deal scoring logic

Create a deal score from 0 to 100.

Start at 100 and subtract points:

- Red flag score subtracts 4 points per red flag
- Asking multiple above 4.0x subtracts 10 points
- Asking multiple above 5.0x subtracts 20 points
- Rent as % of gross above 15% subtracts 10 points
- Rent as % of gross above 20% subtracts 20 points
- Lease years remaining under 5 subtracts 15 points
- Machine age above 12 years subtracts 10 points
- No card system subtracts 5 points
- No wash-and-fold subtracts 5 points
- No pickup/delivery subtracts 5 points
- No commercial accounts subtracts 5 points

Minimum score is 0. Maximum score is 100.

Display quality label:

- 80–100 = Strong Target
- 60–79 = Worth Underwriting
- 40–59 = Maybe
- 0–39 = Weak
