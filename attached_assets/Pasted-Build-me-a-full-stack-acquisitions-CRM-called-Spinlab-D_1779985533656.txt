LAUNDROMAT RED FLAG CHECKLIST
--------------------------------

Each deal should have a red flag checklist with these items:

- Financials are handwritten
- Seller claims cash income
- No tax returns
- Old machines
- Short lease
- Rent above 20% of gross
- Utilities look too low
- Payroll looks too low
- Owner works many hours
- No card system data
- Bad reviews
- Poor location visibility
- Weak parking
- Equipment near end of life
- Landlord approval uncertain
- Seller financing refused
- Asking price too high
- No clear reason for sale
- Lease assignment unclear
- Revenue declining
- Competition nearby is stronger

Calculate Red Flag Score as the number of checked red flags.

Show risk level:

- 0–2 = Clean
- 3–5 = Caution
- 6–8 = High Risk
- 9+ = Dangerous

--------------------------------
DEAL CALCULATIONS
--------------------------------

Automatically calculate:

- Asking multiple = Asking price / Adjusted net income
- Rent as % of gross = Annual rent / Gross revenue
- Annual rent = Monthly rent x 12
- Rent per square foot = Annual rent / Square footage

Create a simple Deal Score from 0 to 100 based on:

- Clean financials
- Strong lease
- Reasonable asking multiple
- Low rent as % of gross
- Newer machines
- Good location
- Upside from wash-and-fold
- Upside from pickup/delivery
- Upside from commercial accounts
- Low red flag score

Display deal quality:

- 80–100 = Strong Target
- 60–79 = Worth Underwriting
- 40–59 = Maybe
- Below 40 = Weak
